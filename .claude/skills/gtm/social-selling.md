# SKILL: social-selling
# Domain: GTM Inbound/Outbound Hybrid
# When to use: LinkedIn signals → outreach sequences.
# Turns LinkedIn activity into pipeline without cold lists.

## WHAT THIS SKILL DOES
Monitors LinkedIn signals from target accounts and
turns them into personalised outreach moments.
Warmer than cold email. More scalable than manual.

## SIGNALS TO MONITOR (in priority order)

Signal 1 — Post engagement (HIGHEST INTENT)
Target liked or commented on content about your category.
→ They are thinking about this problem right now.
→ Outreach within 24 hours of signal.
Hook: "Saw your comment on [specific post]..."

Signal 2 — Profile view from target account
Someone at a target company viewed your profile.
→ They are curious. Meet them there.
→ Connect with note referencing shared context.
Hook: "Noticed you came across my profile..."

Signal 3 — Job posting signal
Target company posted a job suggesting the pain exists.
(e.g. "Marketing Operations Manager" at a retail chain)
→ They are trying to solve this problem by hiring.
→ Position your product as faster than hiring.
Hook: "Saw you're hiring for [role]..."

Signal 4 — Content they published
Target published a post about a challenge you solve.
→ They are broadcasting the problem publicly.
→ Engage with the content first, then connect.
Hook: "Your post about [topic] hit on exactly..."

## LINKEDIN OUTREACH RULES
Connection note: under 300 characters, no pitch.
First message after connect: value-add only, no ask.
Second message: soft intro to what you do.
Third message: CTA for call.

Never pitch in a connection request.
Never send a wall of text.
Never use "I" as the first word.

## OUTPUT
For each LinkedIn signal actioned:
Write to memory/vault/output/linkedin-[DATE].md:
- Contact name and company
- Signal type
- Message sent
- Date sent
- Response (update when it comes in)
