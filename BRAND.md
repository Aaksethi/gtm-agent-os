# BRAND — Current Client Configuration

## PRODUCT
AI SaaS platform that unifies marketing operations across 
all store locations for multi-location retail chains.

Core value: One dashboard, all locations, real data.
Key pain solved: Each store currently runs its own 
disconnected marketing — no unified data, no way to 
measure what's working across the chain.

## ICP — PRIMARY BUYER
Company type: Retail chains with 50+ physical locations
Revenue: $500M+ annually
Geography: United States (primary)

Job titles to target (in priority order):
1. Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)
2. VP of Marketing
3. Head of Marketing
4. Director of Marketing Technology

Buying signals — target companies showing these:
- Recent store expansion (new locations opened in last 90 days)
- Job postings for "marketing coordinator" across locations
- Recent funding round or PE acquisition
- News about "digital transformation" or "unified commerce"

Pain points they talk about:
- "Our marketing is inconsistent across locations"
- "We have no idea which store campaigns are working"
- "Each location does its own thing"
- "We can't scale our marketing team"

## ICP — SECONDARY BUYER
Title: Marketing Operations Manager / MarTech Manager
Role: They influence the decision, don't make it.
Approach: Send them content, not demos.

## NO-GO LIST
Never contact:
- Anyone at competitor companies (update as needed)
- Anyone who has previously unsubscribed
- Companies under 50 locations
- Companies under $500M revenue

## MESSAGING — WHAT RESONATES
Lead with: The cost of disconnected marketing at scale.
Proof point: "Chains our size typically lose 15-20% of 
marketing spend to location-level inconsistency."
CTA: 20-minute call, not a demo. Lower commitment.
